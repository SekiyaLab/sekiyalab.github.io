Most technical sites decorate. This one computes.

The field on the front page is a real Gray–Scott system with a visible step counter, a seed and a checksum, because a research institute about computation should be made of it. If a visual exists here, the preference is that it genuinely executes something; if an interaction exists, it should make the idea underneath easier to feel.

The time axis is the part we care about most. Dragging it backward does not play a recording in reverse — the engine restores a snapshot and re-integrates forward, so what you see at step 640 is what was computed at step 640. The site keeps its own time, and lets you keep it too.

That is the standard every future figure on this site will be held to: computed, seeded, replayable, inspectable.
