Sekiya was founded today as one active research institute: computer science, mathematics and computational experimentation, run for the pleasure of finding out.

The strongest of the founder's earlier independent research moved here. Models & Statistics carries the A-series studies and the market-microstructure work. Systems & Security carries an evidence-and-instrumentation programme — the R-00x findings and trace-npm, which keeps its name and its npm identity. Computation carries a deterministic-computing core. Agentic Systems exists as an honest empty shelf: a category with nothing in it yet is not a gap to fill on a schedule.

Earlier work is not deleted, only consolidated — its questions continue here, under fewer rules and better light.

The spirit, not a workflow: discover → experiment → understand → build → show.
