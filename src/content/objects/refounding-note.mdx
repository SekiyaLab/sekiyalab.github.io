Sekiya was re-founded today as the one active research institute: computer science, mathematics and computational experimentation, run for the pleasure of finding out.

The strongest active research from three predecessor identities moved here. Models & Statistics inherits the A-series studies and the market-microstructure work. Systems & Security inherits Ghalvera's experimental core — the range harness, the evidence ledger, the R-00x findings, and trace-npm, which keeps its name and its npm identity. Computation inherits Forge and EMERGENCE. Agentic Systems exists as an honest empty shelf: a category with nothing in it yet is not a gap to fill on a schedule.

Ghalvera and Lathilda remain exactly as they were — preserved institutions, not deleted ones. Their questions continue here, under fewer rules and better light.

The spirit, not a workflow: discover → experiment → understand → build → show.
